The **Report** tab holds two things: the sign-off card, which records who has
approved the project's numbers and how far they have progressed, and the list
of report documents written on top of them. The sign-off sits above the list
because it is what unlocks publishing.

![The Report tab with the sign-off card at step one of seven and the report document list below it](/images/workbench/report-tab.jpg)

## The sign-off ladder

A project walks seven steps from a working draft to an auditable export
bundle. Each step names the role accountable for it:

| Step | What it means | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Draft only** | A working draft only, nothing is confirmed. | AI / analyst |
| **Ready for client query / internal review** | Ready to put in front of the client, or for internal review. | Advisor |
| **Client-confirmed input** | The client has confirmed the inputs are theirs and correct. | Client data owner |
| **Method-approved calculation** | The methodology lead has approved the calculation method. | Methodology lead |
| **Report-ready draft** | The written report draft is ready to circulate. | Report writer |
| **Locked final package** | The final package is locked; the calculation can no longer change. | Authorised approver |
| **Auditable export bundle** | The auditable export bundle has been produced. | System |

Every move is made by a named person and recorded in an append-only history,
shown at the bottom of the card. The assistant is never the approver: a step
cannot be advanced automatically or by AI.

## Advancing a step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Advance">
    The button names the next step, for example **Advance to Ready for
    query**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm who is approving">
    The dialog states what the step means and which role you are approving it
    as. Enter your name (filled in for you), optionally your role and a note,
    then click **Confirm approval**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

![The advance dialog asking for your name, an optional role and a note before recording the approval](/images/workbench/signoff-advance-dialog.jpg)

**Step back** moves the ladder down one step and requires a reason, which is
recorded in the history alongside the move.

Some steps are gated, and the card explains any block beside a button that
starts the fix. Valuation lines written by the assistant must be reviewed by a
person before the ladder can advance at all. Reaching **Method-approved calculation** also
needs every registry row confirmed, every evidence document approved in the
[data room](/projects/data-room), and the reporting methodology locked on the
project's Overview. Open data gaps never block sign-off; they are left out of
the totals by design, and the card says so.

## Locking the calculation

The step into **Locked final package** requires the engine's calculation to be
locked first. When the project is at **Report-ready draft** and the
calculation is still open, the card offers **Lock the calculation**: a dialog
asks for your name and confirms with **Lock it**. Once locked, no number on
the project can change. Unlocking is not on screen yet; only the assistant can
do it, so treat locking as final.

<Warning>
Lock the calculation only when the numbers are final. Every value on the
[Workbench](/projects/workbench) becomes read-only once the project is locked.
</Warning>

## Report documents

Below the sign-off card sits the project's report documents. Each row shows:

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** | The document's name; click it (or **Open**) to open the builder. |
| **Kind** | **Impact report** or **Impact forecast**. |
| **State** | **Draft**, or **Published vN** once a version has been published. |
| **Updated** | When the draft last changed. |

**New report** asks Vellum to write a first draft from the default template,
with every section drawn from the Workbench, then opens it in the
[report builder](/projects/report-builder).

### Archiving a report

A report you no longer need can be archived with **Archive** on its row.
Reports are archived rather than deleted, so a published version is never
lost. Archiving is reversible and takes effect straight away, with no
confirmation:

- The report leaves the list. As soon as the project has an archived report, a
  **Show archived** switch appears above the list; turn it on to see archived
  reports, each marked **Archived**.
- **Unarchive** on an archived row puts it back in the list.
- Every published version stays readable. Opening an archived report that has
  been published shows its newest published version, with a banner saying the
  report is archived.

However many documents a project has, there is only ever one calculation
behind them. Every figure in every document resolves against the same engine
totals, so two reports can never disagree about a number. Publishing a
document never moves the sign-off ladder, and the ladder applies to the whole
project, not to one document; see
[Publishing and exports](/projects/publishing-and-exports) for how the two
meet.

<Note>
A report can be published once the project reaches **Report-ready draft**.
Until then the publish dialog shows the sign-off as a blocker and points back
to this tab.
</Note>
