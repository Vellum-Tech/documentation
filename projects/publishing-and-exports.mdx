---
title: "Publishing and exports"
description: "Freeze a report as a numbered version that never changes, or export it as a Word or PDF file."
---

A draft report becomes something you can hand over in two ways. Publishing
freezes it inside Vellum as a numbered, read-only version. Exporting renders
the draft as a Word or PDF file to send on. Both carry the engine's figures
exactly as the paper shows them.

## Publishing a version

Click **Publish** in the builder's top bar. The dialog states what publishing
does and checks the two gates before the button is enabled:

![The publish dialog showing the blocking gaps and the sign-off step as blockers, with the published by and version name fields](/images/workbench/publish-dialog.jpg)

- **No blocking gaps.** Every blocking entry in the builder's gaps panel must
  be resolved first; the dialog counts what is left and points at the panel.
- **Sign-off at Report-ready draft.** The project's
  [sign-off ladder](/projects/sign-off-and-reports) must have reached
  **Report-ready draft** or later. Publishing never moves the ladder itself;
  move it on the project's **Report** tab, where each step needs a named
  person's approval.

**Published by** must name the person of record (never the assistant), and
**Version name** is an optional label such as Board review. Confirming saves
any unsaved changes, publishes at exactly that revision, and opens the new
version. The server re-runs both gates on the exact content it freezes, so the
dialog's reading is a preview, never the decision.

## What a published version is

A published version is the report as it read at that moment: the text and
every figure, table, list and source, frozen. It lives at its own read-only
page, badged **Published v1**, **Published v2** and so on (with the version
name beside it, when one was given), and carries a provenance line naming who
published it and when, with the words "This version does not change". The
draft stays editable for the next version, and nothing you change in it, or
on the Workbench, ever reaches an already published version. The document's
**State** on the Report tab shows the newest version, and the builder's header
links to it.

## Exporting a file

**Export**, beside **Publish**, renders the draft as a file:

![The export menu with the Export as Word and Export as PDF options](/images/workbench/export-menu.jpg)

- **Export as Word** downloads a .docx: a branded cover page with the report
  title, the organisation, the reporting period, who prepared it and the
  export date, then the body with the same engine figures the paper shows.
  Tables and charts export as tables of their data, and citations become real
  Word footnotes.
- **Export as PDF** renders the same content as an A4 PDF with its own cover
  and branding.

Exports carry the organisation's report branding (colours, fonts and logo)
where it is set, and Vellum's house style otherwise. An export is a picture of
the draft as it reads now; unlike a published version, nothing stops the draft
changing afterwards, so when the file needs to match a permanent record,
publish a version at the same time.

<Note>
Exporting does not require sign-off or a clean gaps panel, so a working draft
can be shared for review before it is publishable.
</Note>
