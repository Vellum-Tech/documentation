---
title: "The report builder"
description: "A full page editor where the report's words are yours and every number is the engine's."
---

Open a report document from the **Report** tab and it fills the page: the
paper in the middle, a contents and gaps rail on the left, and the report's
settings on the right. You write the prose; every figure, table and chart is a
live reference to the engine's calculation. There is no way to type a dollar
onto the page, and the assistant never supplies one.

![The report builder with a drafted report, the contents outline and the gaps panel on the left](/images/workbench/report-builder.jpg)

## The outline rail

**Contents** lists the report's structure: each Heading 1 with its Heading 2s
nested beneath. Click an entry to scroll to it. To restructure the report:

- Drag an entry to a new place in the rail. The whole section moves: the
  heading and every block beneath it, up to the next Heading 1.
- Or put the cursor in a heading and press `Alt+ArrowUp` or `Alt+ArrowDown`.
- One `Cmd+Z` undoes a whole section move.

## Writing with live figures

Type on the paper as in any editor. Headings, lists and quotes have the usual
markdown shortcuts (`#` for a heading, `-` for a bullet list), and selecting
text shows bold, italic, underline, strikethrough and code.

A number from the model is inserted as a **figure**: type `{` (or pick
**Figure** from the `/` menu), search the catalogue, and press Enter. The
figure lands as a pill in your sentence:

![A paragraph with a figure pill showing the total impact resolved to a dollar amount](/images/workbench/figure-pill.jpg)

A pill is not text. It stores a reference to a metric, and the engine resolves
it every time the report is read, so it is always today's figure. If the model
changes under a published number the pill shows the change and offers
**Accept**; a figure that can no longer resolve shows a dash, never a zero.
**Cite a source** works the same way for referencing documents, producing real
footnotes.

## Blocks from the slash menu

Press `/` on an empty line for the block menu. Above the text blocks sit
presets that draw straight from the calculation:

![The slash menu open on the paper with figure, text and heading entries](/images/workbench/slash-menu.jpg)

| Block | What it inserts |
| --- | --- |
| **Table of outcomes** (also activities, SDGs, beneficiaries) | A table of impact values and shares, one row per entry. |
| **Top 3 outcomes** (also SDGs, beneficiaries) | The same table, limited to the top three. |
| **Bar chart of outcomes** (also activities, SDGs, beneficiaries) | Sorted bars with a **Chart** and **Table** toggle. |
| **Evidence status chart** | Accounts by evidence status. |
| **KPI strip** | Total impact with activity, outcome and SDG counts. |
| **SDG impact donut** | Share of impact by SDG. |
| **Impact flow diagram** | Activities flowing to outcomes and SDGs. |
| **Open data gaps**, **Sign-off trail**, **Data points appendix**, **Sources appendix** | Data lists read from the project's registers. |
| **Policy alignment by level** (also by theme, by subject) | The project's policy links with their aligned values, grouped. |

Every cell, bar and dollar in these blocks is resolved by the engine like any
figure pill. The policy alignment block always prints what alignment means (an
assessment, not a finding of compliance) and how much of the total impact
aligns with at least one policy shown.

The same menu also inserts reusable text sections such as **Background**,
**Methodology notes**, **Limitations** and **Disclaimer**: project text that
can be shared by every report on the project or written for this report only.

## Typed numbers

Any number typed into prose is checked: a money amount, a percentage or a
count, small ones and number words included (a year such as 2025 or FY2025 is
fine). Each one gets a wavy amber underline and is a blocking gap, because a
number on the page should come from the engine.

![A sentence with two typed numbers underlined and the Make figure and Ignore controls open beneath the first](/images/workbench/typed-number.jpg)

Click an underlined number to choose what happens to it:

- **Make figure** swaps the typed text for the figure picker, already searching
  for that number, with figures of the same value listed first. Pick one and it
  replaces the typed text as a live figure. Press `Escape` (or click away
  without choosing) and your typed text is put back.
- **Ignore** keeps the number as typed when it is not a quantity at all. Pick
  why: **A name**, **A date or period**, **A reference number (clause,
  section)** or **Other** (which asks you to say why, then **Ignore it**). The
  underline turns into a quiet dotted line and the gap clears.

An ignored number stays on record: click its dotted underline to see who
ignored it, when and why, and **Restore** to bring the gap back. Ignored
numbers are listed under **Numbers left as typed** in the **Sign-off trail**
block, and a published version keeps the list as it stood.

<Note>
Anyone who can edit the report can use **Make figure**. Only Vellum staff can
ignore or restore a typed number, and the assistant never can.
</Note>

## Images

To add a picture, pick **Image** from the `/` menu and choose a file, or paste
or drop a picture straight onto the paper. Pictures must be PNG or JPEG files
of 10 MB or less. Each one is uploaded into the project's
[data room](/projects/data-room) as a document, and the report refers to that
document.

![A selected image block with its resize handle, the Small, Medium and Full width presets, and the caption and description fields](/images/workbench/image-block.jpg)

Click a picture to select it and show its controls:

- **Width**: drag the handle on its right edge, or pick **Small**, **Medium**
  or **Full**. The width is kept as a share of the text column, so the
  picture sits the same way in the editor, a published version, the PDF and
  the Word file.
- **Caption** is optional and prints under the picture.
- **Description (alt text)** says what the picture shows for readers who
  cannot see it. It is required to publish; while it is empty the image is a
  blocking gap.

If the picture's document is later removed from the data room or marked
sensitive, the image becomes a blocking gap, and a reader who is not allowed
to see that document gets nothing printed in its place: no picture, caption or
description.

## Report settings

Click **Report** in the top bar to open the settings rail:

![The report settings rail with the jurisdictions field and the lead policy picker](/images/workbench/report-settings.jpg)

- **Jurisdictions** sets where this report will be read, as codes such as
  AU-NSW. The policy alignment block then shows policies for those places,
  their countries and international ones.
- **Lead policy** names one policy to print first in the policy alignment
  block, with a line saying the report is framed against it.

## Saving and conflicts

The report saves itself as you type. The top bar shows **Saved**, **Unsaved
changes** while a save is pending, or **Not saved** with the reason when a
save was refused (your text is kept on the page either way). If the same
draft was changed in another tab or by someone else, a banner says the report
changed elsewhere, nothing you typed was saved over it, and offers **Reload**
to pick up the latest version.

Vellum also keeps restore points of the draft as you work, so you can read,
compare or go back to an earlier state; see [Draft history](/projects/draft-history).

## The gaps panel

**Gaps** under the outline lists what stands between this draft and
publishing, blocking items first, then a **To review** list of advisories.
Clicking an entry scrolls to the block it concerns, and the same message
appears as a callout beside that block on the paper. Typical blocking gaps:

- A figure, table or chart whose data points name no evidence document or
  URL. The callout links to the [Workbench](/projects/workbench) and the
  registry to bind the evidence.
- A missing **Methodology notes** or **Limitations** section, inserted from
  the `/` menu.
- Compliance wording in prose: writing that the work "complies with" a policy
  is flagged for rewording, because alignment is your assessment and never a
  finding of compliance. This gap has no dismiss; the words must change
  before the report can be published.
- A typed number, worded for example as: "120" looks like a typed number.
  Insert it as a figure from the slash menu, or ignore this occurrence. See
  [Typed numbers](#typed-numbers).
- An image with no description: "Add a description to this image."
- An image that cannot be used, with the reason: "This image's file is no
  longer in the data room.", "This image is not a PNG or JPEG file.", "This
  image is over the 10 MB limit." or "This image can't be shown in a report."

The panel's list is exactly what publishing checks, so there are no surprises
in the publish dialog; see
[Publishing and exports](/projects/publishing-and-exports).
