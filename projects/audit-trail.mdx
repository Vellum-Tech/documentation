The **Audit trail** tab is the project's change history: every recorded change to the project and the records inside it, newest first. It fills itself; there is nothing to switch on and nothing to remember to log.

![The audit trail listing recent changes with timestamps, field changes and who made each one](/images/projects/audit-trail.jpg)

## What each entry shows

Every entry answers who, what and when:

- **When**: the time of the change, shown relative to now.
- **What happened**: a headline such as "Project detail updated", "Document updated" or "Project member added". Created and deleted records are marked as such.
- **Which fields moved**: an update lists each changed field with its old and new value, so you can see exactly what "updated" means.
- **Who**: the person who made the change. An actor that cannot be matched to a person is shown as an id rather than a guessed name, and background processing appears as the system.

## Where the entries come from

The trail is written at the database level, not by the screens you click. That means every change lands in it the same way, whoever or whatever made it: an edit on the Overview cards, a value typed into the Registry, a change made by the [Vellum agent](/chat/working-with-the-agent) during a conversation, or a record touched by background processing such as file ingestion. If the data changed, the trail says so.

The scope is the whole project: its own details, membership changes, documents, Registry rows, gaps, model entities, valuation lines and sign-off events all report here.

<Note>
The trail is read-only. Entries cannot be edited or removed, which is the point: it is the record you reach for when a number in a report needs explaining months later.
</Note>

## Audit trails elsewhere

Projects are not the only records with a trail. An organisation's record carries the same feed for changes scoped to the organisation itself; see [Organisations](/organisations/overview). Governance decisions on a project, such as locking or unlocking the reporting methodology, are additionally recorded against a named person on the [project record](/projects/project-record).
