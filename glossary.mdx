A short reference for the terms you will meet across Vellum. Each entry links to the guide that covers it properly.

## The people and places

**Organisation.** A client your firm measures impact for. Each organisation has a profile, people, goals, contacts and its own data room. See [Organisations](/organisations/overview).

**Project.** One measurement engagement for an organisation, with a period, a stage, a data room, a Workbench calculation and its reports. Also called an engagement. See [Projects](/projects/overview).

**Stage.** Where a project sits in its lifecycle, from Discovery through Data collection and Drafting to Published. See [the projects list](/projects/overview).

**Vellum staff.** Your firm's team. Staff see every organisation and project. Client members see only their own organisation and the projects they are added to. See [Roles and permissions](/admin/roles-and-permissions).

**Owner, Editor, Viewer.** The three project roles. The owner shares the project and can change everything; editors can read and change; viewers can read everything and change nothing. See [Sharing and access](/projects/sharing-and-access).

**Library curator.** A staff member with the grant that lets them activate or retire Policy library entries. See [Team](/admin/team).

## The model

**Activity.** Something the organisation does in the world, such as delivering home energy audits. Activities seed from the [Activity library](/libraries/activities).

**Beneficiary.** The people or groups who experience the change, drawn from the [Beneficiary library](/libraries/beneficiaries).

**Outcome.** The change an activity creates for a beneficiary, phrased from the [Outcome library](/libraries/outcomes) and aligned to the UN Sustainable Development Goals.

**Impact account.** One row on the Workbench: an activity, a beneficiary and an outcome, valued together. The full worksheet is the Chart of Impact Accounts. See [the Workbench](/projects/workbench).

**SDG.** One of the 17 UN Sustainable Development Goals. Outcomes align to SDGs, and totals can be read by SDG.

## The evidence

**Data room.** Where a project's evidence lives: uploaded files, notes and documents, plus the Registry, gaps and the model entities. See [the data room](/projects/data-room).

**Datapoint.** A figure Vellum extracted from a table in an uploaded document, waiting to be promoted into the Registry.

**Registry.** The register of every data point, assumption and factor a formula uses, each with an owner, a status and its evidence. See [the data room](/projects/data-room).

**Gap.** A recorded piece of missing evidence: what is needed, why, and what has been asked of the client. Open gaps block a report from quietly resting on evidence that was never provided.

**Assumption.** A value used without measured evidence, recorded honestly as such in the Registry.

## The numbers

**The engine.** The deterministic calculator that computes every figure in Vellum. Nobody types a dollar figure onto a report, and the assistant never produces one.

**Factor.** A published reference value (a price, a rate, a wage) with a source citation and version history, held in the [Factor library](/libraries/factors). Reports keep the version they used.

**Formula.** An engine-governed calculation from the [Formula library](/libraries/formulas). Its inputs are operands.

**Operand.** A labelled input a formula needs, such as "cost per household, in AUD". The Workbench binds each operand to measured data or a factor.

**Valuation methodology.** The curated method a valuation follows, from the [Valuation methodology library](/libraries/valuation-methodologies).

**Reporting methodology.** The report-level methodology a project chooses before valuing outcomes, recorded with a rationale and lockable on the project record. See [the project record](/projects/project-record).

**Investment.** The money put into the work, recorded on the project so the report can relate impact to investment. See [the project record](/projects/project-record).

**Total impact.** The engine's headline figure for a project, visible on the Workbench totals panel.

## The report

**Report document.** A draft written in the report builder, either an Impact report or an Impact forecast. See [Sign-off and reports](/projects/sign-off-and-reports).

**Figure pill.** A live number in the report that resolves from the engine. Figures are inserted, never typed. See [the report builder](/projects/report-builder).

**Typed number.** A money amount, percentage or count typed into a report's prose instead of inserted as a figure. It blocks publishing until it is made a figure or, by Vellum staff, ignored with a reason. See [the report builder](/projects/report-builder#typed-numbers).

**Sign-off.** The governed ladder of approvals that locks the calculation before publishing. See [Sign-off and reports](/projects/sign-off-and-reports).

**Published version.** A frozen, read-only copy of a report ("Published v1"), unchanged even as the draft moves on. See [Publishing and exports](/projects/publishing-and-exports).

**Restore point.** The report draft as it stood at a moment, kept automatically every 10 minutes of editing and before each restore, publish and assistant drafting run. It can be opened, compared and restored. See [Draft history](/projects/draft-history).

**Policy alignment.** The recorded links between a project's work and government acts, policies and programmes from the [Policy library](/libraries/policies), shown on the Workbench sub-sheet and in the report's policy block.

**Audit trail.** The read-only, field-level history of changes on organisations and projects. Entries cannot be edited or removed. See [the audit trail](/projects/audit-trail).
