Vellum has one layout everywhere: a sidebar on the left for moving between
areas, a page header with the actions for that area, and a floating chat
button in the bottom right corner.

![The Home dashboard with the sidebar, the Getting started checklist and the Continue, Needs attention and Jump in cards](/images/get-started/dashboard.jpg)

## The sidebar

The main items at the top of the sidebar take you to each area of Vellum:

| Item | What you'll find there |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Your dashboard: pick up where you left off and see what needs attention. |
| **Organisations** | The organisations you work with, each with its profile and data room. |
| **Projects** | Every engagement, from discovery through to the published report. |
| **Libraries** | Your firm's shared catalogues: outcomes, factors, methods, surveys. |
| **Chat** | The Vellum assistant in a full page, with your conversation history. |

Near the bottom of the sidebar sit two more entries: **Settings** opens your
[settings page](/get-started/account-and-appearance), and **Support** opens a
new email to the Vellum team in your mail app. Below them, the footer shows
who you are signed in as; click it to open the user menu.

### Collapsing the sidebar

Click the panel icon at the top of the sidebar to collapse it to a rail of
icons, which keeps more room for working surfaces like the Workbench. Hover
over an icon to see its name, and click the panel icon again to expand it.

![The app with the sidebar collapsed to a narrow rail of icons](/images/get-started/sidebar-collapsed.jpg)

## The Home dashboard

**Home** is a work desk, not a report. It shows four cards:

| Card | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Getting started** | A first-run checklist that ticks itself off as your workspace takes shape. It disappears once complete, or when you click **Dismiss**. |
| **Continue** | The projects you are most likely to pick up next, each with its organisation, period and current stage. **All projects** takes you to the full list. |
| **Needs attention** | Light signals worth clearing: organisations with no projects yet, projects missing a report type, and agent actions waiting on you. |
| **Jump in** | Shortcuts into **Organisations**, **Projects** and **Libraries**. |

Each row in these cards is a link straight to the record it mentions.

## Page headers and create buttons

Every page has a header naming where you are, with that page's actions on the
right. On **Home**, that is **New organisation** and **New project**; the
Organisations, Projects and Libraries pages carry their own create buttons in
the same spot. On a project, the header also holds the stage, sharing and
project actions. See [The project record](/projects/project-record).

## The chat button

The round button in the bottom right corner is **Open chat**. It is on every
page and slides the Vellum assistant in from the right, aware of the page you
are on, so you can ask about the record in front of you without leaving it.
For the full page version and what the assistant can do, see
[Chat](/chat/overview).
