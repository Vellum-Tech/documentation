description: "Sign in to Vellum, switch between light and dark themes, and manage your account."
---

Your account controls live in two places: the user menu at the bottom of the
sidebar for quick switches, and the **Settings** page for the fuller view.

## Signing in

Open Vellum in your browser and you land on the sign-in page. Sign in with
**Google**, **Microsoft**, or by entering your **Email address** and clicking
**Continue**: Vellum then verifies the address and signs you in.

![The Vellum sign-in page with Google, Microsoft and email options](/images/get-started/sign-in.jpg)

There is nothing to install and no password to manage if you use Google or
Microsoft; use whichever account your invitation was sent to.

If you were invited as a member of a client organisation, or you belong to
more than one organisation, your first sign-in may finish on an organisation
picker: choose the organisation you want to start in, and Vellum takes you to
its projects. Which organisation you pick never changes what you are allowed
to see; access is set by your membership. See
[Roles and permissions](/admin/roles-and-permissions).

## The user menu

Click your name and avatar at the bottom of the sidebar to open the user
menu. It confirms the name and email you are signed in with and holds two
controls:

![The user menu open with the Theme submenu showing Light, Dark and System options](/images/get-started/user-menu.jpg)

- **Theme**: choose **Light**, **Dark**, or **System**. System follows your
  device's own light or dark setting.
- **Log out**: ends your session and returns you to the sign-in page.

## The Settings page

Click **Settings** near the bottom of the sidebar to open your settings page.

![The Settings page showing the Appearance and Account cards and the Team card](/images/get-started/settings.jpg)

**Appearance** offers the same **Light**, **Dark** and **System** choices as
the user menu. As the card says, it chooses how Vellum looks on this device:
the theme is saved in your browser, so your laptop and your phone can each
keep their own.

**Account** shows who you are signed in as and your email address, with a
**Sign out** button that works the same as **Log out** in the user menu.

<Note>
If you have the staff role you also see a **Team** card, which shows how many
people are active and leads to the page where you invite people and manage
access. See [Team](/admin/team).
</Note>
