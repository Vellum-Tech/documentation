---
title: "Your first project"
description: "Go from an empty workspace to a valued impact report in four steps."
---

Vellum turns an organisation's evidence into a valued impact report. This page
walks the same path as the **Getting started** checklist on your Home
dashboard: create an organisation, start a project for it, choose a report
type, then value outcomes on the Workbench.

![The Getting started checklist on the Home dashboard with completed steps crossed out](/images/get-started/checklist.jpg)

The checklist ticks itself off as your workspace takes shape, and disappears
once every step is done. You can also remove it at any time with **Dismiss**.

<Steps>
<Step title="Create an organisation">

An organisation is the client you measure impact for. Everything else (projects,
documents, reports) hangs off an organisation record.

From **Home** or **Organisations**, click **New organisation**.

![The New organisation dialog with fields for name, legal name, type, status, sector and website](/images/get-started/new-organisation-dialog.jpg)

Only **Name** is required. The other fields are optional here and stay
editable on the organisation's record:

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | The everyday name you know the organisation by. |
| **Legal name** | The registered entity name, if different. |
| **Type** | The entity type, such as a charity or company. |
| **Status** | Where the relationship stands. |
| **Sector** | The sector the organisation works in. |
| **Website** | The organisation's website address. |

As you type a name, Vellum checks for existing organisations that look
similar and shows them as links. It is a hint, not a block: you can still
create the new record.

Click **Create organisation**.

<Note>
Creating organisations is a staff action. If you are a member of a client
organisation, your advisor sets this up for you. See
[Roles and permissions](/admin/roles-and-permissions).
</Note>

</Step>
<Step title="Start a project">

A project is one engagement: a reporting period, a model, and the report that
comes out of it. From **Home** or **Projects**, click **New project**.

![The New project dialog with an organisation picker, a title field and period dates](/images/get-started/new-project-dialog.jpg)

Choose the **Organisation** the project belongs to and give it a **Title**.
The **Period start** and **Period end** dates default to the current
Australian financial year (1 July to 30 June); change them to match your
reporting period.

Click **Create project**. Vellum opens the new project's record, starting at
the Discovery stage. See [The project record](/projects/project-record) for a
tour of what is on it.

</Step>
<Step title="Choose a report type">

The report type tells Vellum what kind of deliverable the project is working
towards, such as an Impact report.

On the project's **Overview** tab, find the **Details** card and click
**Edit**. Choose a **Report type**, then save.

<Tip>
The **Getting started** checklist links this step straight to a project that
is still missing a report type, and the **Needs attention** card on Home
flags each one with "Report type not set".
</Tip>

</Step>
<Step title="Value outcomes on the Workbench">

The Workbench is the project's working surface: it holds the model of
activities, beneficiaries and outcomes, and the calculation sheet where each
outcome is given a dollar value backed by evidence.

Open the project's **Workbench** tab. Build the model, record the measured
numbers, and bind each outcome's formula to a reference factor or client
data. The engine computes the dollars from there, and your first valued
totals appear on the project.

This step is where most of the work happens, and it has its own guide: see
[The Workbench](/projects/workbench). The reference factors and outcome
definitions it draws on live in your firm's [libraries](/libraries/overview).

</Step>
</Steps>

Once a project has valued outcomes, the checklist completes and your Home
dashboard switches to day-to-day mode: resume work from **Continue** and
clear anything in **Needs attention**.
