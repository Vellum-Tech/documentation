---
title: "Archive and trash"
description: "Archive organisations you are not working with, move them to trash, restore them, and read the audit trail."
---

When work with an organisation winds down you have two options. Archiving tucks it away but keeps it reachable; moving it to trash takes it out of circulation until someone restores it. Nothing on this page permanently deletes an organisation.

## Archive an organisation

Open the organisation and click the **Organisation actions** menu (the three dots at the top right), then **Archive**. There is no confirmation step; the change applies straight away and a toast confirms it. You can also archive from an organisation's row menu on the list page.

![The organisation actions menu open on a sample organisation, showing archive and move to trash](/images/organisations/actions-menu.jpg)

Archiving changes the organisation's status to **Archived**:

- It disappears from the Organisations list until you click **Show archived**, which appears in the toolbar whenever archived organisations exist.
- The record itself stays fully open: its projects, data, and history are untouched.
- The status pill on the record reads **Archived**.

![An archived organisation record with the archived status pill in the header](/images/organisations/archived-record.jpg)

To bring it back, open the same menu and click **Unarchive**. The organisation returns to the status it had before it was archived.

## Move an organisation to trash

<Note>
Moving organisations to trash and restoring them from the Trash page are staff actions.
</Note>

From the **Organisation actions** menu, click **Move to trash**. A confirmation dialog explains what happens before anything changes.

![The move to trash confirmation dialog explaining the organisation can be restored at any time](/images/organisations/trash-confirm.jpg)

A trashed organisation is removed from the Organisations list and from search. It is not deleted: it waits in the trash until someone restores it. Right after trashing, the toast offers a one-click **Restore**.

## The Trash page

Click **Trash** at the top of the Organisations list to open the staff trash page. Each entry shows when it was deleted, with a **Restore** button that returns the organisation to the list.

![The organisation trash page with one sample organisation and its restore button](/images/organisations/trash-page.jpg)

When nothing is waiting, the page shows **Trash is empty**.

## The audit trail

Every organisation record keeps a history of its own changes on the **Audit trail** tab.

![The audit trail tab showing recent field level changes with who made each one](/images/organisations/audit-trail.jpg)

The tab has three sections:

- **Recent changes** lists field-level edits to the organisation record itself: a status change, a new contact, an edited profile field, each with when it happened and who did it.
- **Projects & everything else** covers every other recorded change under the organisation, whoever made it and however it was made.
- **Earlier (reconstructed)** shows history inferred from the record's current state, from before change tracking was captured. Filter buttons (**All**, **Summary**, **Documents**, **Sources**, **Notes**, **Goals**) narrow this section.

For the audit trail inside a project, see [Project audit trail](/projects/audit-trail).
