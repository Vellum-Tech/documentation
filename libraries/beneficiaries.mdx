A beneficiary entry is a governed name for a group a programme serves, such
as "Low income households in private rental". Alongside the wording, each
entry carries a directness classification and, where the group needs
protecting, explicit consent safeguards.

## The beneficiary list

Open **Libraries → Beneficiary library**.

![The beneficiary library list with three groups, their directness and statuses, one marked with a sensitive shield](/images/libraries/beneficiaries-list.jpg)

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Beneficiary group | The governed name, with a shield icon when the group is sensitive |
| Directness | Direct, Indirect, System or Environment |
| SDGs | The Sustainable Development Goals the group relates to |
| Observed | How many times the group has been observed in project work |
| Status | Draft, Curated or Archived |

Search by name and filter by **Directness** or **Status**. Directness keeps
different kinds of claim distinguishable: a **Direct** group is served by the
work itself, an **Indirect** group benefits downstream, **System** covers
changes to a system rather than people, and **Environment** covers claims
about the natural environment.

## Consent safeguards

The consent safeguards are two fields on every beneficiary entry:

- **Sensitive**: a flag marking that consent, privacy controls and careful
  aggregation apply before anything about this group is displayed publicly.
- **Safeguard / permission note**: the caveat an analyst must see before the
  group lands in a report, for example that only aggregated, de-identified
  responses may be used.

On a sensitive group's record, the safeguard note is shown as a warning
banner at the top, above everything else, and the list marks the group with
a shield icon, so the caveat is in front of the analyst before the group is
used anywhere.

![A sensitive beneficiary record with the safeguard banner, details and safeguards cards](/images/libraries/beneficiary-record.jpg)

## The beneficiary record

- **Beneficiary details**: the group name, directness and SDGs.
- **Safeguards**: the sensitive flag and the safeguard or permission note,
  editable with **Edit** on the card.
- **Authoring hints**: example activities and common outcomes for this group.
  These are suggestions surfaced when a project builds its accounts; they are
  never applied automatically.
- **Notes**: context, caveats, or how the group is worded.

The status pill next to the title promotes a tidied group from **Draft** to
**Curated**, or archives it.

## Create a beneficiary group

<Note>
Creating and editing beneficiary groups is staff only. The **New
beneficiary** button does not appear for client organisation members.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the dialog">
    In **Libraries → Beneficiary library**, click **New beneficiary**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the group">
    Enter the group's name and, optionally, its directness and SDGs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the safeguards">
    If the group is sensitive, tick **Sensitive** and write the safeguard
    note an analyst must read before using the group in a report.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it">
    Click **Add beneficiary**. It lands as a draft; promote it to
    **Curated** from its record once settled.
  </Step>
</Steps>
