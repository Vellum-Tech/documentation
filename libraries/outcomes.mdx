title: "Outcome library"
description: "Governed aligned-outcome phrases that seed impact accounts, each with SDG alignment, notes and a curation status."
---

An outcome is a governed phrase describing a change beneficiaries experience,
worded once and reused everywhere: "Household energy costs are reduced", not
five slightly different ways of saying the same thing. Projects draw on these
phrases when building their impact accounts, so the same outcome reads the
same way across clients and reports.

An outcome entry is a phrase, not a figure. The values attached to outcomes
live in the [Factor library](/libraries/factors) and
[Formula library](/libraries/formulas), and the engine computes them.

## The outcome list

Open **Libraries → Outcome library**.

![The outcome library list with three outcome phrases, SDG badges and statuses](/images/libraries/outcomes-list.jpg)

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Outcome | The governed phrase |
| SDGs | The Sustainable Development Goals the outcome aligns with |
| Observed | How many times the phrase has been observed in project work |
| Status | The curation status |

Search by phrase and filter by **Status**. Hover a phrase and click the
pencil to rename it in place; press `Enter` to save or `Escape` to cancel.
Select rows with the checkboxes to archive outcomes in bulk.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Draft | Suggested wording, not yet vetted |
| Curated | Tidied and approved as the governed phrasing |
| Archived | Retired from use |

## The outcome record

Click a row to open the outcome.

![An outcome record showing the phrase, its SDG alignment and notes](/images/libraries/outcome-record.jpg)

- The status pill next to the title is the curation control: click it to
  promote a tidied phrase from **Draft** to **Curated**, or to archive it.
- **Outcome details** holds the phrase and its SDG alignment, editable with
  **Edit** on the card.
- **Notes** records context, caveats, or how the outcome is worded.
- A footer shows when the outcome was added and by whom.

## Create an outcome

<Note>
Creating and editing outcomes is staff only. The **New outcome** button does
not appear for client organisation members.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the dialog">
    In **Libraries → Outcome library**, click **New outcome**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the phrase">
    Enter the outcome phrase. Word it as the change the beneficiary
    experiences, not as an activity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Align and annotate">
    Optionally pick the SDGs the outcome aligns with and add notes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it">
    Click **Add outcome**. It lands as a draft; promote it to **Curated**
    from its record once the wording is settled.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How a project picks an outcome

When a project builds its impact account chains, it draws on the curated
phrases in this library so its outcomes align with the firm's governed
wording, and the **Observed** count on the list reflects how often a phrase
appears in project work. See [Workbench](/projects/workbench) for where
outcomes carry value in a project.
