title: "Activity library"
description: "Governed activity phrases that seed impact accounts, each classified by impact domain and SDG alignment."
---

An activity is a governed phrase describing what a programme does: "Home
energy efficiency upgrades delivered", "Community energy literacy workshops".
Like outcomes, activities are wording, not figures; curating them once means
every project describes the same work the same way when it builds its impact
accounts.

## The activity list

Open **Libraries → Activity library**.

![The activity library list with three activity phrases, their impact domains and statuses](/images/libraries/activities-list.jpg)

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Activity | The governed phrase |
| Domain | The impact domain the activity belongs to |
| SDGs | The Sustainable Development Goals it aligns with |
| Observed | How many times the phrase has been observed in project work |
| Status | The curation status |

Search by phrase and filter by **Domain** or **Status**. The view options
button lets you group the list by either one. Statuses work exactly as in the
outcome library: **Draft**, **Curated** and **Archived**, and the status pill
on an activity's record is where you promote or archive it. Bulk archive is
available from the list's checkboxes.

The impact domain is a governed classification (for example "Climate, energy
and carbon" or "Education, training and employment") that groups related
activities across clients.

## Create an activity

<Note>
Creating and editing activities is staff only. The **New activity** button
does not appear for client organisation members.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the dialog">
    In **Libraries → Activity library**, click **New activity**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the phrase">
    Enter the activity phrase, worded as the work delivered.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Classify it">
    Optionally pick the impact domain and the SDGs it aligns with.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it">
    Click **Add activity**. It lands as a draft; promote it to **Curated**
    from its record once the wording is settled.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The activity record

Click a row to open the activity. The record mirrors the outcome record: an
**Activity details** card with the phrase, domain and SDGs, a **Notes** card,
the status pill next to the title, and a footer recording when it was added
and by whom.
