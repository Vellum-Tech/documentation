A reporting methodology is the report-level frame chosen before any outcome
is valued: it sets the pathway the whole report follows. Individual outcomes
are then valued by the curated
[valuation methodologies](/libraries/valuation-methodologies), calculated
through [Formula library](/libraries/formulas) formulas, and every figure is
computed by the engine.

The library is curated and browse-only. It currently offers the **Vellum
Methodology**, Vellum's own rules in words.

## The library

Open **Libraries → Reporting methodology library**.

![The reporting methodology library listing the Vellum Methodology](/images/libraries/reporting-methodologies-list.jpg)

Each row shows the methodology's name, what it is, who it is best used for
and its typical outputs. Click a row for the full record.

## The Vellum Methodology

![The Vellum Methodology record with its approach, data requirements and outputs](/images/libraries/reporting-methodology-record.jpg)

The record describes the methodology in the same terms the engine actually
works:

- **Description**: Vellum's own way of measuring and valuing a programme's
  social and environmental impact over the reporting period.
- **Core approach**: each outcome is valued in dollars by a formula whose
  every input traces to evidence; value is split across beneficiaries,
  outcomes and the SDGs by recorded weights; only value created within the
  reporting period is counted, for the people actually reached; where an
  outcome has no data, its share of the cost of delivery stands in, marked
  as a fallback.
- **Best used for**: annual impact reports for charities, foundations and
  corporate programmes that want every figure traceable to its source.
- **Core data required**: activities and their financial input, the
  beneficiaries reached, outcome data, and the evidence behind each figure.
- **Typical outputs**: total impact; impact by activity, outcome, beneficiary
  and SDG; return on investment; and an evidence trail for every figure.
- **Strengths and limitations**: every figure is traceable and reconciles to
  the cent; it does not yet apply deadweight, attribution, displacement or
  drop-off, and it values one reporting period rather than a lifetime.

The record also bridges down a layer: a reporting methodology sets the report
pathway, individual outcomes are valued by the curated valuation
methodologies, calculated through Formula library formulas, and every figure
is computed by the engine.

## Choosing a methodology on a project

A project chooses its reporting methodology on the project record, before
outcome valuation begins. See
[Project record](/projects/project-record).
