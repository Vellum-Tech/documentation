Welcome. Vellum turns an organisation's real-world evidence into audited, defensible impact reports. These guides cover every screen: the organisations you work with, the projects that measure their impact, the Workbench where evidence becomes dollar figures, and the libraries that hold your firm's reusable knowledge.

Vellum is built around one rule: **no person ever types a dollar figure onto a report**. Every number is computed by a deterministic engine, and every input traces back to a sourced reference value or the client's own measured data. The built-in assistant can propose and explain, but it never produces a number either.

## How work flows

A typical engagement moves through six steps, and the app walks you through them:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Gather the evidence">
    Upload the client's documents to the [data room](/projects/data-room). Vellum reads them, summarises each one, and extracts the figures it finds into datapoints.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the model">
    Describe what the organisation does: activities, the people who benefit, and the outcomes that follow, seeded from the [governed libraries](/libraries/overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record the numbers">
    Promote evidence into the [Registry](/projects/data-room), where every data point, assumption and factor carries an owner, a status and its source.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Value it in the Workbench">
    Bind evidence and [factors](/libraries/factors) to formulas on the [Workbench](/projects/workbench). The engine computes the figures.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the Registry">
    Resolve the open [gaps](/projects/data-room), confirm the inputs, and work through [sign-off](/projects/sign-off-and-reports).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate the report">
    Write the story in the [report builder](/projects/report-builder), where every number is a live figure from the engine, then [publish and export](/projects/publishing-and-exports).
  </Step>
</Steps>
