Welcome to Vellum — the platform that turns an organisation's real-world evidence into audited, defensible impact reports.

<Note>
  Vellum is built around one golden rule: **no person ever types a dollar figure onto a report**. Every number you'll see in a Vellum report is computed by a deterministic engine, and every input to that computation traces back to either a sourced reference value or the client's own measured data.
</Note>

These guides explain how that works in practice, screen by screen.

## One picture to keep in mind

A **Reporting methodology** sets the style of report you're writing. A **Methodology** is a formula whose inputs — called **operands** — are labelled empty jars ("cost per visit, in AUD"). A **Factor** is a full jar with the same label, a value and a citation. A **Quantified output** is a jar the client filled themselves with measured evidence. The Workbench pours the full jars into the matching empty ones, the engine does the arithmetic, and the result lands on the report with its full audit trail attached.
